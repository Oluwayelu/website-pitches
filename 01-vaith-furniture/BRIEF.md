## The pitch angle
6,700 people follow them, but a buyer who Googles "furniture store Ikeja" finds nothing, and every product is buried in a Facebook feed with no prices, no categories, and no way to browse. The site is an **online showroom/catalogue**: browse by category, tap "Order on WhatsApp", and every item gets a shareable link.

## Design direction
- **Mood:** warm, tactile, premium-but-attainable showroom. Think Article.com / West Elm, adapted to Lagos.
- **Palette:** warm ivory `#F6F1EA` background · deep walnut `#3B2A20` text/primary · brass accent `#B88A44` · soft sage `#8A9A84` for tags. Product photos sit on ivory cards with soft shadows.
- **Type:** headings **Fraunces** (soft serif), body **Inter**.
- **Logo:** SVG wordmark "VAITH" in Fraunces semibold with letter-spacing, plus "FURNITURE · IKEJA" small caps underneath. Use the Facebook profile picture only as a favicon fallback.
- **Signature detail:** a "Made in Lagos, built from scratch" craft section showing the royal-chair build process as a 3-step photo strip (frame → upholstery → finished).

## Site map (single page + catalogue page)
1. **Hero:** full-bleed best sofa photo. H1: *"Furniture that makes your home feel finished."* Sub: *"Sofas, accent chairs and office pieces, built and delivered from Ikeja, Lagos."* CTAs: **Shop on WhatsApp** (wa.me/2347084510703) · **Browse the catalogue**.
2. **Proof strip:** "6.7K followers on Facebook" · "100% recommend (11 reviews)" · "Delivery across Lagos" (phrase it as "Delivery available"; don't claim a coverage area) · "In-store pickup in Ikeja".
3. **Shop by category:** Sofas & sectionals · Accent & royal chairs · Office furniture · Custom orders. Cards use their photos.
4. **Featured pieces grid** (8–12 items from `assets/`): image, short name (e.g. "L-shape fabric sofa"), **"Price on request"** pill (no prices are verified), and an "Order on WhatsApp" button that pre-fills: *"Hi, I'm interested in the [item] I saw on your website."*
5. **Built from scratch:** the craft story plus the 3-step strip. Copy: *"Every piece is built by our own craftsmen, from frame to finish. Want something custom? Send us a picture and your space size."*
6. **How ordering works:** 1) Pick a piece or send your idea · 2) Confirm details & delivery on WhatsApp · 3) Delivered, or collect in Ikeja.
7. **Reviews:** placeholder block "Your 11 Facebook reviews go here" (we don't have the text).
8. **Contact:** WhatsApp, both phones, email, "Ikeja, Lagos" (no street address is verified, so no map pin; a city-level map or none). Hours: unknown, so omit.
