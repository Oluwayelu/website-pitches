## The pitch angle
Their strength is **education**: renters and buyers trust them because they "teach before you pay". The site turns those episodes into a proper **articles hub** (SEO gold: "Lekki rent tips", "what to check before renting in Lekki") next to a clean listings section, replacing a 2021 Blogspot that undercuts their credibility.

4. **Listings:** cards with "Price on request" unless a price is in the post. Include the blog's "Self-contain for rent – Ilasan" as an *archived example* only if Ife confirms it's still relevant; otherwise use placeholders.
