## The pitch angle
Parents are choosing where to make memories of their child. A Google Form doesn't look like a premium studio. The site shows the **themed sets and finished photos up front**, explains packages, and books a date in three taps, while still working with the WhatsApp/phone workflow they use now.

## Design direction
- **Mood:** joyful and premium, like a children's book designed by a luxury brand. Playful, never cheap.
- **Palette:** cream `#FFF8EE` · candy coral `#FF6F61` · sunshine `#FFC94A` · sky `#7CC6FE` · ink `#1F2A44`. Plenty of cream space so the colours pop.
- **Type:** headings **Baloo 2** (rounded, friendly), body **Nunito**.
- **Logo:** wordmark "kids photostudio" in Baloo 2 lowercase, with a small camera icon whose lens is a balloon.
- **Signature detail:** soft confetti/balloon shapes drifting slowly in the hero (CSS/SVG, reduced-motion safe), and photo cards with a slight polaroid tilt.

## Site map
1. **Hero:** collage of 3 birthday-set photos. H1: *"Birthday photos your child will treasure forever."* Sub: *"Themed kids' shoots, family portraits and more, in our studio at Ikeja City Mall."* CTAs: **Book a shoot** · **See our sets**.
2. **Proof strip:** "Inside Ikeja City Mall" · "830+ families following" · "Photo + video".
3. **Shoot types** (cards): Kids birthday shoots ⭐ · Family & adult portraits · Maternity & newborn · Pre-wedding · Model & product · Events & video.
4. **Our sets:** gallery grouped by theme where images allow, with lightbox.
5. **Packages:** 3 tiers as **placeholders** ("Starter / Signature / Party + Video. Prices on request"). Don't invent prices.
6. **How booking works:** 1) Pick a date & shoot type · 2) Confirm on WhatsApp / call · 3) Come to the studio and smile!
7. **Booking form** (name, child's age, shoot type, preferred date, phone) → opens WhatsApp prefilled, with a secondary link "Prefer the old form?" → their Google Form.
8. **Reviews:** placeholder.
9. **Find us:** Ikeja City Mall map embed (Obafemi Awolowo Way, Alausa: the mall itself is a verified public landmark), phone, email.
