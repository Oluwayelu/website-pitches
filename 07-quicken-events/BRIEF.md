- **Mood:** editorial wedding luxury. Think Vogue Weddings: big imagery, restrained type, black/ivory with gold.
- **Palette:** ivory `#FAF7F2` · onyx `#111111` · champagne gold `#BFA06A` · blush accent `#E9D6CF`.
- **Type:** headings **Italiana** or **Bodoni Moda** (display), body **Lato**.
