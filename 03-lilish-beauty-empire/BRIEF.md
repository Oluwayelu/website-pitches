## Design direction
- **Mood:** soft luxury, feminine but grown-up. Think Glossier meets a boutique spa.
- **Palette:** blush `#F4D9D3` · deep plum `#3E1F33` · champagne gold `#C8A97E` · cream `#FFF9F4`. Plum hero overlay, blush section bands.
- **Type:** headings **Cormorant Garamond** (italic accents), body **DM Sans**.
- **Logo:** wordmark "Lilish" in Cormorant italic with a small crown/tiara mark over the "i", plus "BEAUTY EMPIRE" in spaced small caps.
