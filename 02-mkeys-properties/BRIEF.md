## The pitch angle
2,000+ listings are buried in an Instagram grid that nobody can search by area, price or bedrooms. Serious buyers, especially diaspora buyers, want a site they can trust, filter and share. The site turns their feed into a **searchable listings portal** with a sold track record.

## Design direction
- **Mood:** confident luxury real estate. Think Knight Frank / Sotheby's Realty, but warmer.
- **Palette:** deep navy `#0E1B2C` · warm gold `#C9A15B` · off-white `#F7F5F0` · slate `#5B6676`. Photography-led, navy overlay gradients on the hero.
- **Type:** headings **Playfair Display**, UI/body **Manrope**.
- **Logo:** wordmark "MKEYS" + a small key-shaped "K" ligature, with "PROPERTIES LIMITED" beneath. Show "RC 3802584" in the footer (trust signal).
